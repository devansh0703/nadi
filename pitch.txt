NADI — 5-MINUTE PITCH (just read this out loud)

[0:00 - THE HOOK]

Okay, quick show of hands — how many of you own a smartwatch?

Now keep your hand up if you actually wear it every day... Yeah. That's what I thought.

We spend 15,000 rupees on a watch to tell us our heart rate, we wear it for 2 weeks, then it dies in a drawer next to our 2022 New Year resolutions.

So we asked a stupid question: what if you didn't need the watch at all? What if your phone could just... look at you and know your heartbeat?

That's Nadi. Nadi means "pulse" in Hindi. And that's literally what it does.

[0:40 - THE MAGIC TRICK]

Let me show you how stupid-simple this is. I open Nadi. I tap Heart. I look at my phone.

That's it. No strap. No watch. No finger clip. I'm just... sitting here, being handsome, staring at my phone — and it's reading my pulse.

How? Every time your heart beats, blood rushes to your face, and your skin absorbs a tiny bit more green light. You can't see it. I can't see it. But the camera can.

Nadi tracks your forehead and cheeks 30 times a second, averages that green signal, runs it through a full DSP pipeline — normalization, Hamming window, FFT — finds the dominant frequency between 45 and 180 BPM, and then — here's the flex — a quantized INT8 neural net running ON the Hexagon NPU cleans up all the motion noise.

Face tracking with MediaPipe. Signal math in native C++ with ARM Neon. AI refinement on the NPU. All in like 3 seconds. All on-device.

It's basically a doctor, but make it selfie.

And it doesn't just give you heart rate. It gives you stress from HRV, live waveform, signal quality — like "bro, move toward the window, your lighting is trash." Plus full history, saved on your phone.

[2:00 - BUT WAIT, THERE'S A SECOND APP INSIDE]

Here's where it gets unfair. Nadi is actually TWO apps in one.

Tab two: Workout. And this one uses zero camera. Nothing. Nada. It's all sensors.

You put your phone in your pocket and do push-ups. Squats. Sit-ups. Jumping jacks. Deadlift. Bench press. Overhead press. Nadi counts every rep from the accelerometer.

It calibrates to YOU in the first 15 samples — learns your baseline, smooths the noise, then counts a rep only when the signal spikes, peaks, and falls back, with a cooldown so a bounce doesn't get double-counted. Push-ups on the Z-axis, squats on the Y-axis, barbell lifts with a gravity-tracking filter because heavy reps are slow and sexy.

You know what's my favorite feature? The elliptical mode. It's just a timer. Start. Stop. Elapsed time. Because unlike every fitness app that makes up numbers, Nadi refuses to pretend it can measure what it can't. Honest app. Rare breed.

And running? Oh, running is beautiful. GPS distance with haversine, live pace, route polyline with 5000 points, cadence from your bounce — a step is anything over 11.5 meters per second squared with 250ms spacing. And there's an AI mode that AUTO-STARTS when you start striding and AUTO-PAUSES when you stop to tie your shoe and rethink your life. Elapsed time freezes. No fake stats.

[3:20 - THE PRIVACY MIC-DROP]

Now, the part investors and your mom both love: privacy.

Everything I just described? On. Device. No cloud. No account. No "we value your privacy" while uploading your face to a server in Virginia.

Your face frames never leave the phone. Your accelerometer data never leaves. Your GPS route that looks like a drunk potato? Stays. On. Your. Phone.

We built this NPU-or-nothing. If the Hexagon NPU isn't there, the app says "sorry, not supported" instead of silently falling back to some slow, leaky cloud workaround. That's a design principle, not a bug. Your body data is yours.

[3:50 - THE FUTURE — WHERE THIS GOES]

And we're just getting started. We're layering an on-device AI coach — Qwen running right on the same NPU, fully offline.

Imagine: "Why is my heart rate higher today?" — and it answers from YOUR last 7 days, not WebMD telling you you're dying.

"Make me a weekly plan, I have dumbbells, 3 days a week, 40 minutes." Done. Only exercises the app can actually count. Adaptive — "you crushed last week, +1 set, here's why."

Food photo → "that's chole bhature, 550 to 650 calories, protein is crying, add paneer." Built for India — veg, non-veg, Jain, sattvic, budget tiers. Protein-gap tracking because let's be honest, vegetarian diets need help.

Scan a blood report → pulls Hb, glucose, HbA1c, B12, Vitamin D. AQI-aware coaching — "AQI 310, bro, workout indoors today." Hindi + English first, then Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi. Elder-care mode with big text and voice-first. Wellness reports as PDFs you can WhatsApp to your mom or your doctor.

All offline. All private. All on a phone.

So here's the pitch in one line:

Fitbits cost money. Subscriptions cost money. Trainers judge you.

Nadi needs two things you already have: your face, and your pocket.

Stare at your phone. Get your heart rate. Put it in your pocket. Get jacked. Stay private.

Nadi. Your pulse. In your pocket.

Thank you. I'm going to go for a run now — the app already auto-started it.
