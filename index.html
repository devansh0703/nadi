Why Nadi?

Contactless Vitals

Measures pulse and HRV remotely using rPPG from the front camera. No wearable and no contact — just your face in frame.

Sensor-Counted Workouts

Push-ups, sit-ups, squats, jumping jacks, deadlift, overhead press and bench press counted from the accelerometer alone — no camera in the loop.

Runs & Routes

Duration, distance, pace, cadence and a live route trace for normal and cadence-aware AI runs. Elliptical runs as honest manual timed mode.

Body Signals

Chest respiration, sternum heart rate and HRV, hand-tremor screening and step readouts pulled from the phone's inertial sensors.

Arm Optimized

Arm Neon SIMD signal processing in C++17, with the 1D CNN refinement running on the Hexagon NPU through Qualcomm SNPE.

Privacy First

100% on-device processing. Camera frames and motion data are analysed locally and never uploaded anywhere.

System Architecture

A high-performance pipeline optimized for Arm processors, processing frames at 30FPS with sub-10ms latency.

Vision Pipeline

MediaPipe Face Landmarker isolates ROI (Region of Interest) on the cheeks and forehead to extract raw PPG signals.

Native DSP

Custom C++ layer uses Arm Neon Intrinsics to process circular buffers, reducing CPU load by 40%.

AI Refinement

A 1D CNN model running on the Hexagon NPU (via Qualcomm SNPE / DLC, INT8) cleans motion artifacts from the heart rate signal — tuned for the iQOO 15's Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5.

Motion Pipeline

Workout reps are counted from a single accelerometer axis using hysteresis peak detection and gravity-referenced lift tracking, with a calibration baseline per set.
